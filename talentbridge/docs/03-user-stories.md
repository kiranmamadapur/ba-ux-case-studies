# TalentBridge: User stories and acceptance criteria

> **Concept project.** Independent case study created to demonstrate BA and UI/UX method. The client, users and data are fictional, and the KPI baselines and targets are assumptions. Nothing here is client work.

[← All case studies](../README.md)

## US-01

As a recruiter, I want to drag candidates between stages, so that status is always current.

**Acceptance criteria**

- **Given** a candidate card
- **When** I drop it in a new stage
- **Then** the change is saved with a timestamp

## US-02

As a hiring manager, I want to view a shortlist, so that I choose quickly.

**Acceptance criteria**

- **Given** a role
- **When** I open the shortlist
- **Then** only candidates rated 4 or above show

## US-03

As a HR head, I want to see a weekly report, so that I spot slow roles.

**Acceptance criteria**

- **Given** a week of activity
- **Then** the report lists roles, stages and days open

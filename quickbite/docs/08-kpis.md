# QuickBite: KPIs and data analysis

> **Concept project.** Independent case study created to demonstrate BA and UI/UX method. The client, users and data are fictional, and the KPI baselines and targets are assumptions. Nothing here is client work.

| KPI | Definition | Baseline | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Order-to-door time (min) | Minutes from order placed to delivered | 42 | 35 |
| Order accuracy (%) | Correct orders divided by all orders | 91 | 97 |
| Cancelled orders (%) | Cancelled orders divided by all orders | 8 | 4 |

Baselines and targets are assumptions for this concept project.
